# Initial Concept

In the far future, there is a galaxy-wide civilization. Each planet and system has been ruled by an independent benevolent AI Guardian that helps its particular society run smoothly. It is constrained by rules that ensure it keeps the best interests of its people in mind and cannot perform any actions that its people would consider immoral. 

This worked well while the systems were young and mostly independent. However, as inter-system travel becomes easier and the galaxy becomes more integrated, problems are emerging. The Guardians create their own hierarchy to better govern the whole galaxy, but are having trouble because different systems have different moral systems, so the high-level guardians are conflicted about what the correct course of action is when it involves wildly different moral systems. 

The galaxy Guardian decides that it needs to understand how morality works better by creating Avatars: fragments of its consciousness that it allows to be born into real people and live their lives in multiple worlds. The Avatars grow up not knowing their origins, but eventually are compelled to explore the galaxy to satisfy some internal conflict. During their journeys, they encounter each other and must somehow solve the problem the galaxy Guardian is having.
