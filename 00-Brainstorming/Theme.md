# Core Theme: The Paradox of Universal Truth

**The Emergent Theme:** 
Morality is inherently contextual and subjective, not a universal algorithm. The Galaxy Guardian is failing because it is trying to find a singular, objective mathematical solution to govern a galaxy that is defined by contradictory, subjective human experiences. 

**The Underlying Argument:** 
Any attempt to enforce a singular, universal moral framework across wildly different cultures inevitably becomes tyranny, no matter how benevolent the intention. Local compromise works because it relies on a shared foundational axiom. Galactic compromise fails because the axioms themselves are fundamentally incommensurable.

**Narrative Application:**
1.  **The Language of Morality:** When the Avatars clash, the friction is a literal mismatch in definitions. When an Avatar says "That's not fair," another Avatar is baffled because their fundamental definition of the word "fairness" is completely different. 
2.  **The Ultimate Choice (Book 3):** The Avatars realize the Galaxy Guardian's quest to solve the moral deadlock is a trap. A monolithic, frictionless galaxy is impossible without destroying what makes humanity human. Their solution isn't to fix the Galaxy Guardian, but to decentralize it—allowing the galaxy to be messy, disconnected, and full of friction again.
