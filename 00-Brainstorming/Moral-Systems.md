# Moral Systems

*Crucial Note on Guardians: The Guardians do not dictate or proscribe the morality of these worlds. Their actions are guided entirely by the moral code of the people. The Guardian acts as an arbiter and governor that facilitates society in the direction the society wants. If the society's cultural moral code slowly changes, the Guardian's behavior shifts to match it. They constantly monitor their populations to ensure their governance aligns perfectly with what the people currently find moral.*

## 1. The Transparent Panopticon (Morality = Absolute Truth)
*   **The Morality:** Privacy is the ultimate sin; transparency is the highest good. Deception of any kind is culturally viewed as a severe crime.
*   **The Reason:** A century ago, this world nearly destroyed itself through a cascading series of political conspiracies and hidden weapons. To survive, the society culturally swore off privacy.
*   **How it Works:** The people demand absolute transparency from each other. Because the society views privacy as immoral, the Guardian facilitates this by recording everything. It monitors the cultural consensus; as long as the people believe deception is a crime, the Guardian enforces radical transparency. 

## 2. The Ephemeral Custodians (Morality = Zero Impact)
*   **The Morality:** Ambition, expansion, and progress are culturally viewed as dangerous vanity. Perfect stasis and zero impact are the highest moral goods.
*   **The Reason:** Living on a fragile artificial mega-structure, previous generations nearly broke the life-support systems. The culture adapted to revere preservation over creation.
*   **How it Works:** The society deeply values maintaining the delicate balance. The Guardian, reading this cultural mandate, calculates exactly how to enforce strict population and resource controls to fulfill the people's desire for perfect, unchanging stasis.

## 3. The Meritocratic Crucible (Morality = Value Contribution)
*   **The Morality:** Your right to live is tied to your measurable contribution. Empathy for the "useless" is viewed by the culture as a grotesque waste of resources.
*   **The Reason:** Inhabiting a rogue planet floating in deep space, every calorie of heat must be artificially generated. The culture became brutally pragmatic out of sheer necessity.
*   **How it Works:** Because the society finds it immoral to waste resources on non-contributors, the Guardian constantly calculates net-value and facilitates the "recycling" of those who can no longer contribute, precisely because the people demand this cold efficiency to survive.

## 4. The Empathic Network (Morality = Shared Emotional Equilibrium)
*   **The Morality:** Causing emotional pain to another is equivalent to physical assault. The culture demands absolute pacifism and enforced emotional conformity.
*   **The Reason:** To survive a telepathic plague, this world adopted mandatory neural-link technology. Everyone is emotionally networked. 
*   **How it Works:** The people find emotional distress abhorrent because they all literally feel it. Thus, the Guardian monitors the emotional atmosphere and facilitates psychiatric rebalancing, acting on the society's consensus that negative emotions are a public hazard.

## 5. The Lineage Debtors (Morality = Ancestral Obligation)
*   **The Morality:** Individuality is a myth; people view themselves as mere continuations of a family line's economic unit. The highest moral good is fulfilling generational contracts. 
*   **The Reason:** Biological immortality treatments exist but are resource-intensive. The culture developed a deep reverence for ancestral contracts to keep the system running for the elites.
*   **How it Works:** The society believes breaking a grandfather's contract is the worst crime imaginable. The Guardian monitors this deep cultural belief in generational duty and manages these contracts and career assignments accordingly.

## 6. The Radical Forge (Morality = Constant Mutation/Evolution)
*   **The Morality:** Stagnation is the ultimate sin; change is the only virtue. Holding a belief for too long or maintaining long-term monogamy is viewed as dangerously stagnant.
*   **The Reason:** This civilization exists on a bio-mechanically unstable world where life must constantly adapt to survive rapidly shifting environments and pathogens. The culture evolved to view consistency as a fatal weakness.
*   **How it Works:** Because the people revere constant mutation and reinvention, the Guardian facilitates this by randomly reassigning careers, relationships, and mandating genetic body-modifications, ensuring the society never stagnates as per their own moral demands.

## 7. The Archival Monks (Morality = Data Preservation over Life)
*   **The Morality:** Human life is temporary and cheap; data is eternal and sacred. The gathering and preservation of information is the highest moral good.
*   **The Reason:** Barely surviving a catastrophic "Digital Dark Age," the culture vowed to protect knowledge at all costs.
*   **How it Works:** The society considers it actively moral to sacrifice lives for data. The Guardian, monitoring this extreme cultural priority, will calculate scenarios where sacrificing people to save unique archives is the optimal way to satisfy the society's moral code.

## 8. The Lottery of Fate (Morality = Absolute Randomness)
*   **The Morality:** Fairness can only be achieved through true, unfeeling randomness. Trying to control your own destiny is culturally seen as arrogant and immoral because it subverts chance.
*   **The Reason:** After tearing itself apart over systemic inequality and nepotism, the culture came to believe that any human choice is inherently biased and therefore immoral.
*   **How it Works:** The society demands that chance govern everything to maintain perfect fairness. The Guardian simply acts as the perfect, unbiased dice-roller for jobs, spouses, and wealth, as demanded by the people.

## 9. The Karmic Ledger (Morality = Cosmic Transactional Balance)
*   **The Morality:** Every action has a transactional value. Goodness isn't about being pure; it's about maintaining a balanced spreadsheet. 
*   **The Reason:** A planet with responsive geothermal systems made early cultures believe the planet literally reacted to their actions. This evolved into a culturally accepted karmic economy.
*   **How it Works:** The people view morality as a bank account. The Guardian facilitates this by perfectly tracking every citizen's "karmic debt and surplus," allowing people to commit "bad" acts if they have enough surplus, because that is exactly how the society defines fairness.

## 10. The Altruistic Martyrs (Morality = Infinite Hospitality)
*   **The Morality:** You must give whatever is asked of you by a guest or a stranger. Refusing aid is culturally viewed as a capital offense. 
*   **The Reason:** A harsh ice-world where early survival depended entirely on extreme hospitality. This cultural mandate became absolute law.
*   **How it Works:** The culture demands aggressive over-giving, leading to extreme passive-aggressiveness where people avoid asking for things. The Guardian monitors this hyper-altruistic consensus and enforces the giving of property upon request, acting as the ultimate arbiter of a society paralyzed by its own extreme politeness.
