# Brainstorming: The Blind Spots

The "Blind Spots" are unregulated zones where the Guardian's sensors are broken or jammed, meaning people living there cannot earn merit but also cannot be immediately recycled. Here are three high-concept directions for how these zones operate:

## Option 1: The Feral Engine (Parasitic Survival)
* **The Vibe:** Brutal, desperate, and territorial. Think *Mad Max* in the dark. 
* **The Location:** The deepest, most irradiated maintenance shafts and exhausted mining veins near the planet's core.
* **How They Survive:** They act as literal parasites on the Crucible. They physically tap into the main atmospheric and nutrient pipes to siphon off what they need before the Guardian registers the pressure drop. 
* **The Economy:** It's a violent gang-state. They trade smuggled, scavenged parts (and illicit substances, like painkillers or synthesized adrenaline) to desperate people up on the "Grid." In exchange, Grid-dwellers "accidentally" drop official rations into the shafts.

## Option 2: The Analog Renaissance (The Sanctuary of the Inefficient)
* **The Vibe:** Tragic, beautiful, and fiercely human. 
* **The Location:** Old, sealed-off habitation domes that the Guardian deemed too mathematically inefficient to maintain, but which still retain pockets of ambient geothermal heat.
* **How They Survive:** They aren't criminals; they are runaways. It's a sanctuary for "Burdens," artists, and those whose Syndicates collapsed. They survive through illegal, hyper-efficient fungal farming and extreme, self-imposed rationing. 
* **The Economy:** They deal in the ultimate contraband: unquantifiable human experience. They run hidden speakeasies where stressed Grid-dwellers sneak in to hear actual acoustic music, see unapproved art, or just experience unmonitored human touch and empathy, paying for it with smuggled Guardian rations.

## Option 3: The Ghost Syndicates (Systemic Subversion)
* **The Vibe:** Cold, calculating, and highly dangerous. A hacker mafia.
* **The Location:** The Blind Spots here aren't static places; they are a shifting network of dead zones. The "Ghost Syndicates" intentionally break or jam Guardian sensors to create temporary, moving shadows to operate within.
* **How They Survive:** They are the ultimate black market, and they don't just trade physical goods—they trade *Merit*. They have figured out how to spoof the Guardian's Ledger using analog bypasses.
* **The Economy:** Grid-dwellers who are on the verge of being recycled (or want to artificially boost their Syndicate's status) come here to buy stolen Multiplier points or fake Forge records. The price is usually terrifying—like selling a healthy family member into the Ghost Syndicates, or agreeing to act as a saboteur for them on the Grid.
