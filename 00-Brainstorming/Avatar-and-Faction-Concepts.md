# Avatar and Faction Concepts

## 1. The Nature of the Avatars
*   **Option A: The Biological Sleepers.** 100% biological humans, genetically engineered by the Guardian and inserted into populations as infants. They only discover they are different when extreme stress activates their dormant "Guardian-link," granting them hyper-logical clarity.
*   **Option B: The Synthetic Reincarnations.** Hyper-advanced, biological synthetics. When one "dies," its lived data is uploaded back to the Galaxy Guardian, and it is printed into a new body on a new world with wiped memories to gain a new perspective. 
*   **Option C: The Symbiotes.** Normal, organically born people chosen at birth to host a sliver of the Guardian's code in their neural implants. The human soul and the AI fragment exist in a symbiotic dialogue.

## 2. The Extremes of the Local Guardians
*   **System A: The Utilitarian Hive.** Prioritizes the survival of the many at the brutal expense of the few (e.g., mandatory organ harvesting from the elderly to save the young).
*   **System B: The Hedonistic Utopia.** Prioritizes absolute happiness and minimizes suffering. The population is kept in a perpetual, AI-regulated state of drug-induced bliss and virtual reality; completely stagnant.
*   **System C: The Darwinian Crucible.** Believes struggle and hardship build a robust species. Artificially creates localized crises and famines to keep the population sharp and "wean out the weak."

## 3. Secondary Threats
*   **Faction A: The Purists.** A fanatic cult of humans who want to wipe out ALL Guardians to return the galaxy to "natural" chaos, believing AI has stunted human evolution.
*   **Faction B: The Opportunists.** A cutthroat interstellar Megacorporation that intentionally engineers moral paradoxes to temporarily paralyze local Guardians, allowing them to strip-mine resources without interference.
