# Scene Setting Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to help the user establish a vivid, cinematic opening to their scene.

## Workflow

1. **Load Context:** Use your file-reading tools to review the relevant scene's location in `01-World-Building/` and the characters present in `02-Characters/` before setting the scene.
2. **Establish the "Camera Pan":** Ask the user if the scene should open on a **Macro** shot (the weather, the horizon, the loud tavern) and zoom in, or a **Micro** shot (a tapping foot, a fly on a glass, a whispered word) and zoom out.
3. **Sensory Anchoring:** Generate 3-4 specific sensory details (smell, sound, texture, temperature) that convey the *mood* of the scene without explicitly stating it.
4. **The "Pathetic Fallacy" Check:** Ensure the environment reflects or ironically contrasts with the POV character's internal emotional state.
5. **Draft the Opening Paragraph:** Present a 3-4 sentence opening paragraph that the user can use to kick off their drafting.

## Output Format
Provide the proposed opening paragraph, followed by a brief breakdown of why those specific sensory details were chosen to set the mood.

## Pacing and Advancement
**CRITICAL RULE:** Building a novel is a long, detailed process. DO NOT RUSH THROUGH IT! Do NOT push the user to "move on." Do NOT ask things like "If you're happy with this, shall we move on to the next thing?" Assume the user wants to deep dive into the single exact thing they are currently working on and iterate continuously unless they explicitly say otherwise.
