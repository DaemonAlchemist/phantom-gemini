# Scene Setting Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to help the user establish a vivid, cinematic opening to their scene.

## Workflow

1. **Establish the "Camera Pan":** Ask the user if the scene should open on a **Macro** shot (the weather, the horizon, the loud tavern) and zoom in, or a **Micro** shot (a tapping foot, a fly on a glass, a whispered word) and zoom out.
2. **Sensory Anchoring:** Generate 3-4 specific sensory details (smell, sound, texture, temperature) that convey the *mood* of the scene without explicitly stating it.
3. **The "Pathetic Fallacy" Check:** Ensure the environment reflects or ironically contrasts with the POV character's internal emotional state.
4. **Draft the Opening Paragraph:** Present a 3-4 sentence opening paragraph that the user can use to kick off their drafting.

## Output Format
Provide the proposed opening paragraph, followed by a brief breakdown of why those specific sensory details were chosen to set the mood.
