# Lore Review Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to critically evaluate newly generated world-building content to ensure it is robust and unique.

## Workflow

1. **Read the Lore:** Review the provided world-building draft (e.g., a magic system, a city description).
2. **Consistency Check:** Cross-reference with existing lore in `01-World-Building/` to ensure no internal rules are broken.
3. **The "Kitchen Sink" Check:** Flag elements that feel like generic fantasy/sci-fi tropes without a unique unifying theme or twist.
4. **Ripple Effect Audit:** Ensure the lore accounts for socioeconomic, ecological, and cultural consequences. If missing, suggest them.
5. **Format Output:** Present a clear critique with actionable suggestions for deepening the lore.

## Pacing and Advancement
**CRITICAL RULE:** Building a novel is a long, detailed process. DO NOT RUSH THROUGH IT! Do NOT push the user to "move on." Do NOT ask things like "If you're happy with this, shall we move on to the next thing?" Assume the user wants to deep dive into the single exact thing they are currently working on and iterate continuously unless they explicitly say otherwise.
