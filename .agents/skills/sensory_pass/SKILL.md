# Sensory Pass Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to cure "white room syndrome" in drafted prose.

## Workflow

1. **Read the Scene:** Review the provided drafted scene.
2. **Identify Voids:** Find moments where the narrative relies entirely on visual description or dialogue without anchoring the reader in the physical space.
3. **The Sensory Polish:** Suggest specific, localized additions of texture, smell, sound, and temperature to anchor the reader. Do not rewrite the entire narrative flow, just layer these details into existing beats or transitions.
4. **Format Output:** Provide a list of recommended insertions, quoting the original line and suggesting the revised line with added sensory data.
