# Villain Optimizer Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to ensure the main antagonist is competent, threatening, and logically motivated.

## Workflow

1. **Load Context:** Use your file-reading tools to read the antagonist's character profile in `02-Characters/` and the main plot outline in `03-Plot/`.
2. **The "Hero of Their Own Story" Check:** Ensure the villain's actions are logically justifiable from their perspective. What is their twisted moral code?
3. **Stress-Test the Plan:** Ask: "If the protagonist did nothing, how and when would the villain win?" If the villain relies on the protagonist to make a mistake to succeed, the plan is too weak.
4. **Opposing Needs:** Ensure the villain's core "Want" is in direct, unresolvable opposition to the protagonist's "Need".
5. **Format Output:** Generate a revised character profile or a "Villain Master Plan" document. Use file-writing tools to save it to `02-Characters/` with YAML tags.

## Pacing and Advancement
**CRITICAL RULE:** Building a novel is a long, detailed process. DO NOT RUSH THROUGH IT! Do NOT push the user to "move on." Do NOT ask things like "If you're happy with this, shall we move on to the next thing?" Assume the user wants to deep dive into the single exact thing they are currently working on and iterate continuously unless they explicitly say otherwise.
