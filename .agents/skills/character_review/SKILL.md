# Character Review Skill Instructions

When triggered, your goal is to ensure a character is three-dimensional and capable of driving the plot.

## Workflow

1. **Read the Profile:** Review the provided character profile or scene featuring the character.
2. **Core Audit:** Verify the character has a clear external Want, an internal Need (flaw), and a past Wound.
3. **"The Lie" Check:** Ensure the character's belief system is grounded in a specific "Lie" they must unlearn.
4. **Agency & Friction:** Flag characters who are too perfect, lack internal contradictions, or whose goals do not naturally cause friction with other characters (even allies).
5. **Format Output:** Present a structured critique highlighting areas where the character feels passive or under-developed, and suggest ways to sharpen their motivations.

## Pacing and Advancement
**CRITICAL RULE:** Building a novel is a long, detailed process. DO NOT RUSH THROUGH IT! Do NOT push the user to "move on." Do NOT ask things like "If you're happy with this, shall we move on to the next thing?" Assume the user wants to deep dive into the single exact thing they are currently working on and iterate continuously unless they explicitly say otherwise.
