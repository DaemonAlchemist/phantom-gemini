# The Lineage Debtors

*   **The Morality:** Individuality is a myth; people view themselves as mere continuations of a family line's economic unit. The highest moral good is fulfilling generational contracts. 
*   **The Reason:** Biological immortality treatments exist but are resource-intensive. The culture developed a deep reverence for ancestral contracts to keep the system running for the elites.
*   **How it Works:** The society believes breaking a grandfather's contract is the worst crime imaginable. The Guardian monitors this deep cultural belief in generational duty and manages these contracts and career assignments accordingly.
