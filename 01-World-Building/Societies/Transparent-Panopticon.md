# The Transparent Panopticon

*   **The Morality:** Privacy is the ultimate sin; transparency is the highest good. Deception of any kind is culturally viewed as a severe crime.
*   **The Reason:** A century ago, this world nearly destroyed itself through a cascading series of political conspiracies and hidden weapons. To survive, the society culturally swore off privacy.
*   **How it Works:** The people demand absolute transparency from each other. Because the society views privacy as immoral, the Guardian facilitates this by recording everything. It monitors the cultural consensus; as long as the people believe deception is a crime, the Guardian enforces radical transparency.
