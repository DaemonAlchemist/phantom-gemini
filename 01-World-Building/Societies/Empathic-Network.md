# The Empathic Network

*   **The Morality:** Causing emotional pain to another is equivalent to physical assault. The culture demands absolute pacifism and enforced emotional conformity.
*   **The Reason:** To survive a telepathic plague, this world adopted mandatory neural-link technology. Everyone is emotionally networked. 
*   **How it Works:** The people find emotional distress abhorrent because they all literally feel it. Thus, the Guardian monitors the emotional atmosphere and facilitates psychiatric rebalancing, acting on the society's consensus that negative emotions are a public hazard.
